## Ground Rules

### Circuit Breaker

**If work doesn't ship by [End Date]:**

- What shipped: Keep it
- What didn't: Re-shape for future or drop

**Extension requires:**
- 90%+ of value delivered
- <20% of appetite remaining
- Explicit team agreement

### Scope Changes

**Agent can:**
- Discover new scopes as work progresses
- Combine/split scopes if makes sense
- Adjust implementation approach

**Agent cannot:**
- Add features outside pitch boundaries
- Extend deadline
- Ship without PR review

### Review Commitment
