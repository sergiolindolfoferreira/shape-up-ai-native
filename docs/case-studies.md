## Example Format

### Company: [Name]

**Team:**
- 2 senior developers (review)
- 3 AI agents (build)

**Cycle:** 1 week

**Adaptations:**
- Daily PR reviews (30 min each)
- Friday deploys only
- Bi-weekly betting (every 2 cycles)

**Results:**
- Shipped 12 features in 8 weeks (was 4 before)
- 85% cycle completion rate
- Average PR review time: 18 hours

**Lessons:**
- Shaping quality matters more than speed
- Review capacity is real bottleneck
- Circuit breaker saved us twice

**Quote:**
> "We thought AI would let us ship 10x more. It's more like 3x, but that's still incredible."
